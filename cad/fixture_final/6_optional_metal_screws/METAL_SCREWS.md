# Assembly with shop screws (instead of the printed pins, clips and straps)

The pictures for this option are in `7_assembly_with_shop_screws/` (shopping list + steps 1-6).
Use the printed **laser_bar.stl** (in this folder) and the printed **angle plates** (folder 3).
The printed pins, forks, laser clip and straps (folder 2) are then not needed.

| Item | Qty (+ spare) | Where |
|---|---|---|
| M3 x 8 mm screw | 4 (+2) | Camera board onto the 4 posts (threads into the plastic) |
| M3 x 25 mm screw | 2 (+2) | Laser bar |
| M3 hex nut | 2 (+2) | In the 2 pockets at the back of the laser holder |
| M3 washer | 6 (+2) | Under every M3 screw head |
| M4 x 16 mm screw | 8 (+2) | 4 camera tilt (through angle plate + cheek + holder) and 4 clamp block (from underneath) |
| M4 hex nut | 8 (+2) | Camera tilt: in the pockets inside the camera holder. Clamp block: in the slots of the block. |
| M4 washer | 8 (+2) | Under every M4 screw head |
| Stainless worm-drive hose clamp, 40-60 mm, band 13 mm wide or less | 2 | Clamp block onto the torch neck (60-80 mm for the thick torch body) |
| Small cable ties | 10 | Cables |

Without the angle plates the 4 camera-tilt screws would be M4 x 12 (the plate adds 2.5 mm).
Fit of every screw and nut was checked against the part geometry: see `7_assembly_with_shop_screws/FIT_CHECK_REPORT.txt`.
